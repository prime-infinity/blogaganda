HOW QLOO HELPED IN ARTICLE SEEDING
===================================

WITHOUT QLOO:
- OpenAI extracts "Leica", "Oura Ring", "British Airways" as text strings
- No way to verify they're real entities (could be hallucinations)
- No way to connect them across articles
- Can't use them for recommendations (just disconnected text)

WITH QLOO:
- Validates entities are real (searched Qloo database, got matches)
- Provides unique IDs (e.g., 75105C50-F33A-4507-BAB8-E15A2A9B4596 for Leica)
- Enables cross-article matching (same entity in different articles = same ID)
- Powers future recommendations (structured data vs raw text)

RESULT:
Qloo turned raw extracted text into structured, validated, connected data that can power 
cross-category taste intelligence and recommendations.
